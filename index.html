02 · Interactive — open itDONE

The Student File

Click a student, land in their File — same world, one student deep. His own stretch of the journey opens the page: the stages he's crossed, where he stands today, and his door already ajar. Every piece of work shows you its opening line and pass count at a glance; hover and it opens in place — the answer, what you said last pass, and what he changed since. Sign off in one click (with a visible undo), and the room itself responds: the light warms as you approach his world, and the page exhales when the work is cleared. The roster is always one keystroke away in the corner.
